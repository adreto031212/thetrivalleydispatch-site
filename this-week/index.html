This week in Tri-Valley.

The dated things to know between now and next Thursday, from issue 1 of the Tri-Valley Dispatch. Every item links its public source.

Heads up

  • Rain across the valley until early Friday, November 20. Dublin's notice, citing the National Weather Service, expects up to 2 inches there between late Tuesday and early Friday. Report street flooding or a downed tree in Dublin at 925-833-6630, in San Ramon at 925-973-2800 (weekdays). Free sandbags: end of this issue. City of Dublin
  • BART runs every 30 minutes Saturday night, November 21, from 10 PM, with one track in the Transbay Tube for lighting work. The Blue Line to Dublin/Pleasanton keeps running; the Red and Green lines stop. BART

What the city decided and what changed

Pleasanton's Hometown Holiday parade fills Main Street on Saturday, December 5, at 5 PM

The parade is followed by a tree lighting in front of the Museum on Main and runs rain or shine.

Livermore shreds paper free this Saturday, November 21, from 8 to 11 AM, at a new spot: Las Positas College, Lot E

Livermore residents can bring up to three banker boxes of documents and stay in the car while they are shredded on site.

Dublin's Tassajara Road work is due to finish by December 1

Phase 1 of the Tassajara Road Improvements is on southbound Tassajara between North Dublin Ranch Drive and Quarry Lane; December 1 is the deadline in the project's regulatory permit.

San Ramon's council takes up a citywide traffic-signal plan on Tuesday, November 24, at 6:30 PM

The updated technology plan covers the city's 109 signals (the city runs 103, Caltrans six): better vehicle detection, more coordinated and adaptive signal timing, and accessible pedestrian signals.
